# NBA 2K Attribute Glossary

This guide explains what each attribute primarily affects in gameplay and simulation.

## Finishing (Scoring At The Rim)

### Driving Layup
Ability to finish layups while moving toward the basket, including eurosteps and acrobatic finishes.

### Standing Dunk
Ability to dunk from a standing position under the rim. Important for big men.

### Driving Dunk
Ability to dunk while attacking the basket with momentum (fast breaks, cuts, drives).

### Close Shot
Ability to make very short shots near the basket (hooks, standing shots, quick push shots).

## Shooting

### Mid-Range Shot
Ability to make jump shots inside the 3-point line.

### Three-Point Shot
Ability to make shots beyond the 3-point line.

### Free Throw
Ability to make free throws at the line.

### Shot IQ
How smart the CPU player is at choosing good shots vs bad shots.

- High Shot IQ: takes open shots.
- Low Shot IQ: forces contested shots.

## Post Scoring

### Post Hook
Ability to score hook shots in the post.

### Post Fade
Ability to score fadeaway jump shots from the post.

### Post Control
How well a player backs down defenders and performs post moves.

## Drawing Contact

### Draw Foul
How often a player gets fouled when shooting or driving.

- High rating: more and-1 opportunities and free throws.

## Ball Handling / Playmaking

### Ball Handle
Controls dribbling ability and how hard it is to steal the ball.

### Speed With Ball
How fast a player moves while dribbling.

### Hands
Ability to catch passes cleanly and hold onto the ball in traffic.

### Pass Accuracy
Controls pass speed and accuracy.

### Pass IQ
How smart the player is at deciding when to pass.

### Pass Vision
Ability to see open teammates, especially for advanced passes.

## Offensive Simulation Ratings

### Offensive Consistency
How consistent a player's scoring is in simulation.

- High rating: performs closer to real averages.

## Defensive Simulation Ratings

### Defensive Consistency
How consistently a player performs defensively in simulation.

## Defense

### Interior Defense
Defense inside the paint (post defense, rim contests).

### Perimeter Defense
Defense against ball handlers and shooters on the perimeter.

### Steal
Ability to strip the ball or intercept passes.

### Block
Ability to block shots.

### Help Defense IQ
How well the player rotates and helps teammates defensively.

### Pass Perception
Ability to read passing lanes and anticipate passes.

## Rebounding

### Offensive Rebound
Ability to grab rebounds on your team's missed shots.

### Defensive Rebound
Ability to grab rebounds after opponent misses.

## Athleticism / Physical Attributes

### Speed
How fast the player runs without the ball.

### Agility
Side-to-side quickness and defensive movement speed.

### Strength
Ability to hold position, absorb contact, and back down players.

### Vertical
How high the player can jump (important for dunks, blocks, rebounds).

### Stamina
How long a player can play before getting tired.

## Misc Attributes

### Intangibles
A hidden rating mainly used to calculate overall rating.

- It does not really affect gameplay.

### Hustle
How aggressively a player goes for loose balls, rebounds, and effort plays.

### Overall Durability
How likely the player is to avoid injuries.

### Potential
How high a player's overall rating can grow in franchise modes.
